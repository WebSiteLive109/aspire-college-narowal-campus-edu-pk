Franchise Requirements

Basic requirements to qualify for Aspire Colleges franchise

  • Educational Background: Minimum Bachelor's degree (preferably in Education or Management)
  • Business Experience: Previous business or management experience preferred
  • Financial Capability: Adequate financial resources for investment and working capital
  • Property Requirements: Minimum 5,000 sq. ft. property in good location
  • Commitment: Full-time commitment to the franchise business
  • Passion for Education: Genuine interest in quality education and community development
