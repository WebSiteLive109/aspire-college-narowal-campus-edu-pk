Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to secure your admission

01

Application Submission

Fill out the online application form or submit a physical form at the campus admission office. Ensure all required documents are attached.

02

Entrance Test

Appear for the college entrance test. Test dates will be communicated after application submission. Preparation guidelines will be provided.

03

Interview

Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview with the admission committee. This helps us understand your academic goals and aspirations.

04

Fee Submission

Upon selection, submit the admission fee within the given timeframe to confirm your seat. Payment plans and scholarships available.

Admission Requirements

Documents and qualifications needed for admission

Academic Documents

  • Matriculation Marksheet (Original + 2 copies)
  • Matriculation Certificate
  • Character Certificate from previous institution
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)

Personal Documents

  • 4 Recent passport-size photographs
  • Student's B-Form/CNIC (copy)
  • Father's/Guardian's CNIC (copy)
  • Domicile Certificate (copy)

Additional Requirements

  • Admission Form (duly filled)
  • Affidavit on stamp paper (as per format)
  • Medical Fitness Certificate
  • Blood Group Report
