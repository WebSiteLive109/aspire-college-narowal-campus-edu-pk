How to Reach Us

Find your way to Aspire Colleges Narrowal

By Road

Located on College Road, Near Narrowal Bypass. Easily accessible from Lahore via GT Road.

  • From Lahore: 2 hours via GT Road
  • From Islamabad: 3 hours via Motorway
  • Parking: Available for visitors

Public Transport

Well-connected through public transport services from major cities.

  • Bus Service: Regular from Lahore & Islamabad
  • Local Transport: Available from Narrowal City
  • College Bus: For enrolled students

Landmarks

Easy to locate with prominent landmarks nearby.

  • Near: Narrowal Bypass
  • Opposite: City Park
  • Adjacent: Sports Stadium
  • Behind: District Hospital
