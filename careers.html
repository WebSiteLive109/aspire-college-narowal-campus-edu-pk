Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about careers at Aspire Colleges

What qualifications are required for teaching positions? +

For lecturer positions, we require a minimum of Master's degree in the relevant subject with B.Ed. or M.Ed. For senior positions, PhD is preferred. Minimum 2-3 years teaching experience at college level is required.

What is the typical hiring process timeline? +

The hiring process typically takes 3-4 weeks from application deadline. This includes application screening (1 week), interviews (1-2 weeks), and final decision (1 week). Urgent positions may have faster turnaround.

Do you provide accommodation for faculty? +

Yes, we provide on-campus accommodation for out-of-town faculty members at subsidized rates. The accommodation includes furnished apartments with basic amenities. Housing assistance is also available for those preferring off-campus living.

What professional development opportunities are available? +

We offer annual training budget for each employee, support for conference attendance, regular workshops, leadership development programs, and opportunities for higher education sponsorship based on performance.

Can I apply for multiple positions? +

Yes, you can apply for multiple positions that match your qualifications. However, we recommend focusing on positions where your skills and experience best align. Each application is considered separately.

Ready to Join Our Team?

If you don't see a suitable position currently open, you can still submit your CV for future opportunities. We're always looking for talented individuals to join our team.
